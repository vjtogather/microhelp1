This course is for software engineers who know C but have never touched a microcontroller, and for hobbyists who want a structured, no-fluff path into embedded development.

By the end, you'll have written and flashed your own GPIO, UART, and timer drivers from scratch — no HAL magic, just registers and datasheets.

## Who this is for

- Software engineers moving into embedded roles
- Students who want hands-on experience beyond Arduino sketches
- Hobbyists ready to go past blinking an LED

## Prerequisites

Basic C knowledge. No prior embedded experience required — we start from "what is a register."
