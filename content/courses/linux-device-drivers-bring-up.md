This is the deep end. You'll take a custom (or emulated) board from "nothing boots" to a working Linux system with your own kernel drivers.

Topics include Device Tree authoring, writing a character driver for a custom peripheral, I2C/SPI driver development, and the full bring-up flow from U-Boot to a booting kernel with your hardware recognized.

## Who this is for

Engineers already comfortable with embedded C and basic Linux usage who want to move into BSP and driver development roles.

## Prerequisites

Completion of *RTOS From Scratch with FreeRTOS* or equivalent experience, plus comfort with the Linux command line.
