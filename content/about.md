---

MichroHelp is a learning platform built for engineers who want to go deep on **embedded systems** — from bare-metal firmware and RTOS internals to driver development and hardware bring-up.

We publish long-form technical articles and hands-on courses aimed at closing the gap between "I can blink an LED" and "I can ship production firmware."

## Why MichroHelp

- **Practical, not theoretical.** Every article and course is built around real boards, real registers, and real debugging sessions.
- **Written by engineers who ship.** Content comes from people who have shipped embedded products, not just read datasheets.
- **Always evolving.** New articles and courses are added regularly as the toolchain and hardware landscape changes.
